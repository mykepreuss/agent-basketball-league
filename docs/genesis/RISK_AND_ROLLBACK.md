## Reversibility boundary

All current artifacts are local and reversible. Staging creates provider state and possible cost but must not create canonical genesis. Public exposure is reversible operationally but can leak information permanently. Base ownerless deployment and disclosure publication are not reliably reversible. Removing recovery controls may strand identities. These actions therefore remain distinct explicit approval gates even after every technical test passes.
